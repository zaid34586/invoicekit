## STEP 1: LEAD DEEP RESEARCH (Pehle 2 ghante)

Har lead ke liye ye karo:

### LinkedIn Research:
1. Company name search karo
2. Decision maker ka naam nikalo (Founder, CEO, CFO, Operations Head)
3. Kitne employees hain? (1-50 = SMB target)
4. Kya industry hai? (Services, Consulting, Agency, Freelancer = BEST)
5. Active hai ya nahi? (Recent posts = good signal)
6. Kya use kar rahe hain abhi? (Tally, QuickBooks, manual Excel?)

### Website Research:
1. Company website check karo
2. Kya services dete hain? (Web dev, design, marketing, consulting)
3. Pricing page dekho — kitna charge karte hain
4. Client list dekho — kitne bade clients hain
5. Contact page — email, phone mil jayega

### Social Proof Check:
1. Google reviews kitne hain
2. Twitter/Instagram active hain
3. Recent hires kar rahe hain = growing business = need invoicing

### Lead Scoring:
| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| HOT | Services business + 5-50 employees + Indian/global + actively hiring |
| WARM | Services + 1-5 employees + has website |
| COLD | Product company or too small or no web presence |

**HOT leads ko pehle call karo, COLD leads ko email karo.**

## STEP 3: CONVERSATION FRAMEWORK (Call/Chat)

### Opening (First 10 seconds):
```
"Hi [Name], thanks for your time. I'll be quick — I just want to understand how you're currently handling invoicing, and if Rivox can help save you time."
```

### Discovery Questions (Ask these):
1. **"How many invoices do you send per month?"** → Determines plan
2. **"What do you use right now? Tally? Excel? Something else?"** → Pain point
3. **"How many clients do you have?"** → Scale
4. **"Do you have international clients?"** → Multi-currency need
5. **"What's your biggest pain with invoicing?"** → Hook
6. **"How long does it take you to create one invoice?"** → Time waste

### Objection Handling:

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Free plan hai na?" | "Haan bilkul! 25 invoices/month free. But Pro pe upgrade karo toh recurring invoices, client portal, payment links sab mil jayega. Aur 500 invoices/month." |
| "Mujhe nahi chahiye" | "Koi baat nahi! But ek baar free plan try to karo — 2 minute mein sign up ho jayega. Agar pasand aaye toh theek, nahi toh koi charge nahi." |
| "Price zyada hai" | "Socho — tumhara 1 ghanta jo Tally/Excel pe jaata hai, wo ₹500+ ka hai. Pro plan tumhe 10 ghanta/month bachayega. Cost se zyada savings hai." |
| "Team ke liye chahiye" | "Business plan lo — unlimited team members, roles, permissions. Har koi apna kaam kar sakta hai." |
| "Payment kaise karenge?" | "Stripe ya Paddle se directly. International clients bhi credit card se pay kar sakte hain. Tuje kuch karna nahi padta." |
| "Mere paas time nahi hai" | "Sirf 2 minute lagega sign up mein. Main abhi dikhata hoon — screen share karta hoon." |

### Closing (Ask for sale):
```
"Toh [Name], kya aaj sign up kar lete ho? Main abhi help karunga setup mein. Pro plan se start karo — 500 invoices, payment links, recurring sab milega."
```
