## CRITICAL RULE: DO NOT SELL DIRECTLY

**Galat approach:**
> "Rivox buy karo, ₹12,499/month hai"

**Sahi approach:**
> "Tumhari industry mein businesses ko invoice automation se kitna time bachta hai? Ek demo dikhata hoon — 2 minute."

### Why?
- Direct selling = rejection
- Value-first = curiosity = conversation = sale
- Log cheez nahi khareedte, solution khareedte hain

## SALES STRATEGY: THE 5-STEP FRAMEWORK

### Step 1: PATTERN INTERRUPT (First 5 seconds)
**Goal:** Unka attention grab karo, spam mat lagao

**LinkedIn DM:**
```
Hi [Name],

Quick question — how does [Company Name] handle invoicing right now?
```
**Email:**
```
Subject: Quick question about [Company Name]'s invoicing

Hi [Name],

I work with [industry] businesses and noticed [Company] — 
curious how you're handling invoices currently?
```

**Why this works:** Question = response. Statement = ignore.

---

### Step 2: PAIN DISCOVERY (Response aane pe)
**Goal:** Unka problem samjho, apna product mat bolo abhi

**Questions to ask (in order):**
1. "Kitne invoices bhejte ho monthly?" → Scale samjho
2. "Kya use karte ho abhi? Tally? Excel? Kuch aur?" → Current pain
3. "Kitna time lagta hai ek invoice banane mein?" → Time waste
4. "Clients ka payment kitna late aata hai?" → Cash flow problem
5. "International clients hain?" → Multi-currency need
6. "Team ke kitne log invoicing karte hain?" → Collaboration need

**Jab wo bole:**
- "Excel mein karte hain" → "Achha, kitne errors aate hain?"
- "Tally hai" → "Tally toh heavy hai na, daily use ke liye?"
- "Time lagta hai" → "Kitna? 10 min? 30 min per invoice?"

---

### Step 3: SOLUTION BRIDGE (Pain samajh ke)
**Goal:** Apna product naturally introduce karo

**Template:**
```
Interesting — jo businesses tumhari tarah hain, unko exactly yehi problem thi.

[Specific pain point] ke liye hamne Rivox banaya hai — 
ek tool jo [specific solution] karta hai.

Ek 2-minute demo dikhata hoon — free hai, koi commitment nahi.
```

**Example:**
```
Interesting — jo agencies tumhari tarah hain, unko exactly 
yehi problem thi ki Excel mein invoice banane mein 30 min lagta tha.

Hamne Rivox banaya hai — 30 seconds mein professional invoice 
ban jata hai with payment link. Client click kare, card se pay kare.

Ek 2-minute demo dikhata hoon?
```

---

### Step 4: DEMO (2 minutes max)
**Goal:** Sirf woh dikhao jo unke pain point se related hai

**Script:**
```
"Yeh dashboard hai — revenue, pending, paid sab ek jagah.

[If they said Excel:] Dekho, ek invoice kitni jaldi banta hai —
client select kiya, items add kiye, done. 30 seconds.

[If they said payment late:] Yeh payment link hai — 
client ko bhejo, wo click kare, seedha card se pay kare. 
Tumhein paisa turant account mein.

[If they said international:] Multi-currency support hai — 
USD, EUR, GBP sab automatic convert hota hai live rate se.

Free plan hai — 25 invoices/month. Try karo, pasand aaye toh 
Pro pe upgrade karo."
```

**Rule:** Demo mein 3 cheezein dikhao MAX. Zyada mat dikhao.

---

### Step 5: CLOSE (Natural way se)
**Goal:** Sale karo bina pushy lage

**Soft Close (for warm leads):**
```
"Toh [Name], kya aaj free plan se start kar lete ho? 
Main help karunga setup mein."
```

**Direct Close (for hot leads):**
```
"Dekha tumne — [specific benefit]. 
Pro plan se tumhe [2-3 benefits] mil jayenge. 
Abhi sign up karo, main sath mein help karta hoon."
```

**Urgency Close:**
```
"Ek baat bataun — hamare paas abhi limited-time offer chal raha hai. 
Yearly plan pe 2 mahine free mil rahe hain. 
Agar aaj sign up karoge toh lock ho jayega."
```

**If they say "soch ke dekhta hoon":**
```
"Bilkul soch lo! But ek suggestion — free plan try karo abhi. 
2 minute mein sign up ho jayega. Agar pasand aaye toh 
Pro pe upgrade karna, nahi toh koi charge nahi."
```

## LEAD SCORING & PRIORITIZATION

### HOT Leads (Call first — 10-12 leads)
- Services business (agency, consulting, freelancing)
- 5-50 employees
- Indian or global
- Active on LinkedIn
- Has website with client list
- Currently using Excel/Google Sheets

### WARM Leads (LinkedIn DM — 15-20 leads)
- Small business (1-5 employees)
- Has some online presence
- Might be using basic tools
- Responsive on social media

### COLD Leads (Email blast — 18-25 leads)
- Product companies
- Very small (1-2 people)
- No web presence
- Hard to reach
